Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Boxpit ("the app") is a Formula 1 news reader, race calendar, and standings app for Android, published by the developer gmlabs. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. It is written to match what the app's code actually does.

Short version

Boxpit does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data the app stores on your device

The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet, and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • The list of news feeds you subscribe to, and your app settings.
  • Articles downloaded from those feeds, and which ones you have read or bookmarked.
  • Cached Formula 1 schedule and standings data.

You can erase all of this at any time using Android's "Clear storage" option for the app, or by uninstalling it.

Network connections the app makes

To show you content, the app connects directly to:

  • The RSS/Atom news feeds you are subscribed to (by default: RaceFans, Autosport, Motorsport.com and PlanetF1 — you can add or remove feeds).
  • api.jolpi.ca, a free public Formula 1 data API, for the race calendar and championship standings.
  • Image servers (content delivery networks) belonging to those news publishers, to load the pictures that appear with articles.

These requests are made over HTTPS. They necessarily reveal your device's IP address and a standard request to those third-party servers, as any web request does; the operators of those services handle that data under their own privacy policies. Boxpit sends them no identifier, account, or personal information of any kind.

Permissions

The app requests only:

  • Internet / network state — to download feeds, F1 data, and images, and to check whether you are online.
  • Run at startup / wake lock / foreground service — used by Android's WorkManager to refresh feeds and the home-screen widget's race schedule in the background so content is ready when you open the app. These do not access any personal data.

Analytics, advertising, and third-party SDKs

There are none. Boxpit contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK. It does not use an Advertising ID.

Children

Boxpit is a general-audience news app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.
